# Mumbai MMR Restaurant Market Intelligence

## Objective

To analyze the restaurant market across Mumbai, Navi Mumbai,
and Thane to understand restaurant distribution, cuisine
preferences, pricing, ratings, and potential market opportunities.

## Research Questions

1. Which areas have the highest concentration of restaurants?
2. How does restaurant density differ across Mumbai, Navi Mumbai and Thane?
3. What are the most popular cuisines?
4. How does cuisine preference vary by location?
5. How does pricing vary across locations and cuisines?
6. Does restaurant price correlate with ratings?
7. Is there a relationship between ratings and review count?
8. Which cuisine-location combinations appear underserved?
9. What market opportunities can be identified from the analysis?
